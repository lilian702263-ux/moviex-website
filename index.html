Install in under a minute

MovieX is distributed as an Android app file (APK), so installation takes four quick steps. No account, no sign-up.

1

Download the APK

Tap any Download button on this page. The file MovieX-1.6.66.apk saves to your Downloads folder.

2

Allow the install

When Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps — this is the normal step for any APK installed outside the Play Store.

3

Tap Install

Open the downloaded file from your notifications or the Files app, then tap Install.

4

Open & watch

Launch MovieX, pick Nova or Classic, and start watching. Updating later works the same way — just install the newer file over the old one.

Questions

Is MovieX free?

Yes — the download on this page is free, and there's no account or sign-up needed to start watching.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

MovieX is shared directly as an APK from this page instead. That's why Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or Files app during setup — it's the standard step for any app installed this way.

Does it work outside Bangladesh?

Yes. MovieX has no geo-lock — it works abroad just as it does at home, on any Android phone or tablet with an internet connection.

How do I update when a new version comes out?

The app itself will let you know. Download the newer APK from this page and install it over your current one — your list, favorites and history stay exactly as they are. Never uninstall first, or you'll lose them.

Can I watch on a bigger screen?

Yes — there's also a MovieX PC edition for Windows, with the same Nova home on your laptop or desktop. This page is for the Android app; ask on the MovieX page for the PC download.
